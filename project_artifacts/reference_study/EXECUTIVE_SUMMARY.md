# Form Architects study — executive summary (Phase A, 2026-11-14)

**What was viewed.**
- 12 of the 18 reels on @formarchitects.iq, stepped through at 40 timecodes, read-only, in the owner's signed-in Chrome.
- The captions of the other 6 reels and of both carousel posts. Their pictures were **not** viewed, and no claim is made about them.
- No audio was assessed.

**Who they are.** A Baghdad studio that builds real-time interactive 3D property apps: aerial, masterplan, floor cut-aways, unit finder, sun and weather, walk mode, delivered as a link. Their output is the nearest real-world benchmark for NAKHLA's live 3D mode. Their picture quality sits between NAKHLA's WebGL view and NAKHLA's Cycles stills.

## Strongest usable insights

1. **Trees carry the realism.** Almost every exterior frame is dominated by a dense canopy filling the gaps between buildings. NAKHLA's district has bare streets.
2. **Parked cars in numbers** make aerial streets read as a lived-in city. NAKHLA's district has none.
3. **Far context can stay plain massing** in haze. They do this too (FA05 fog, FA01 masterplan), which confirms NAKHLA's tiered near / mid / far approach.
4. **Night:**
   - wet reflective paving;
   - irregular warm windows;
   - **uplit trees and lit paths**.
   NAKHLA has the first two and lacks the third.
5. **The unit finder colours units on the tower.** This maps directly onto the new demo pricing: highlight the selected unit on the 3D tower.
6. **People** (a seated woman in an abaya; a man on a bench) add local life. They stay blocked for NAKHLA until the owner approves assets (D12).
7. **Façade:** a deep-set frame grid with balcony slabs. The frame **continues above the roof as an open crown**. NAKHLA's roof ends flat. A crown was previewed and then adopted by the owner (D18, 2026-11-14).

## Confirmed NAKHLA deficiencies (against these references)

- Canopy density in the district.
- No district cars.
- No uplit landscape at night.
- Flat roof line.
- Narrow living-room glazing with no cove light.
- No people.

## Resources and constraints

- **Render protection:** the study ran while a 47-view Cycles batch was rendering. It used one browser tab with paused, muted video and changed no project files the render depended on.
- **Resolution:** Instagram serves these reels at 360 × 640, so material micro-detail cannot be judged.

**Outputs:** the index, shot breakdown, priority map and art-direction options in this folder. The matrix and four-way boards come in Phase B, once the new renders exist.
