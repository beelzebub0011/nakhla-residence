# High-value amenities and architectural risk register

## Level 10: Wellness and recreational level
Program to test, not fixed sizing: welcome/reception, comfortable fitness room, strength/cardio/studio zones, swimming pool/deck, separated changing rooms + showers + sanitary facilities, sauna/steam if economically/regulatorily viable, relaxation lounge, towel/linen/storage, accessible WC and route, safety equipment, janitor/plant/humidity/dehumidification/service zones. Operationally separate wet circulation from gym/restaurant traffic and private residential routes.
Critical unknowns: actual pool dimensions and depth, load, vibration, transfer structure, drainage and leak detection, water treatment, enclosed pool HVAC and vapor barrier/condensation, acoustic transmission, rescue rules, slip-resistant decks, fire separation, occupancy/evacuation, accessible arrival, maintenance access. No unqualified structural load estimates asserted as approved. Show a "structural and MEP engineering review required" note in planning artifacts. If elevated pool proves physically or commercially unrealistic, autonomously evaluate moving it to a low podium or roof-adjacent pool and document ADR; preserve a wow-factor guided 3D tour.
Digital wow-factor: raymarched-like or physically credible water shader if performant, gentle caustics or baked effects, controlled reflections, separation of visible glass and steam, purposeful camera anchors, selectable equipment information cards, facilities gallery. For indoor pool, no unrealistic giant open-air infinity edge if not structurally resolved.

## Level 11: Sky restaurant, café, shisha and terrace
Program: restaurant indoor dining, kitchen with separated service zones (fire and grease), café counter + seating, private panoramic lounge, restrooms, storage, waste, service corridor, manager/host, open-air terrace. Shisha lounge conditional: study jurisdictional legality, licensing, smoking restrictions and whether adult-only controlled area could be permitted, combustion/CO risks, separation from nonsmoking patrons and residential fresh-air intakes. Do not claim mechanical ventilation completely eliminates secondhand smoke exposure. Model as a separate open-air zone with clear signage and no access for minors, or swap concept to non-smoking panoramic lounge if constraints fail. Food/health/fire approvals are outside website model scope.
- Need independent guest/resident access concept, noise control, evening atmosphere without oversaturating lights, weather protection, fall protection and service logistics. Rooftop restaurants need real mechanical and wastewater routing; render believable ducts/plant enclosures at architectural scale.
- UI: switch restaurant/café/terrace zones, time-of-day, gallery carousel, information only. Reservation widget, menu/payment or real contact should be demo-only unless a business owner supplies real backend integration.

## Safety boundaries
This is a fictional real-estate marketing showcase, not signed-off architectural drawings. All safety constraints are research questions and code-compliance gates. No "certified safe", "building approved", "available for sale", "actual photograph", "compliant pool", "legal shisha" claims without evidence. Red flags go to risks register and design alternative, not hidden in pretty visuals.

## Commercial value
The amenities deliver 3D tour variety, differ from repetitive apartments, help real estate company demos of lifestyle. However they should not consume 80% of the initial build time at expense of one excellent exterior and unit. Sequence: functional zones -> one scene quality -> polished later.
