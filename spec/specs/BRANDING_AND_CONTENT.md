Logo should be original vector typographic + subtle palm-derived mark with minimalist construction (not generic cliché filled palm clip art).

Typography: elegant modern Arabic body and display paired with clean Latin typography, license checked. Adjust by visual test/contrast; do not hardcode color where it breaks AA contrast.
