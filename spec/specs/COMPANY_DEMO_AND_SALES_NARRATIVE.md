# Company showcase delivery — how to make the concept commercially convincing

## Company demo mission
A property developer should understand the commercial value in five minutes: buyers can visualize before construction, discover floor and apartment layout, understand and switch finishes, see building amenities, reduce unnecessary sales questions, save interest and share with sales team. Claim this as a *potential workflow*, not quantified ROI unless measured.

## First 20 seconds
Be breathtaking but usable: strong real photorealistic exterior hero, brand and "Explore Building" action visible immediately, no forced wait or unreadable tiny fonts. Show geography visually inspired by Basra (palms, warm sun, urban massing); label setting as concept. One delightful floor hover and animated selection should show capability without needing explanation.

## 60-second walkthrough
Visitor selects 04, clicks 04A, watches cutaway reveal real rooms, enters living and clicks flooring to switch variant, notices material inspector metadata, camera stable and visual material change obvious. Language toggle can happen mid-flow without reset. This should be polished enough to screen record for a sales pitch.

## Five-minute detailed demo
In sequence: hero -> orbit and Day/Night -> Floor 04 -> apartment A/B comparison -> unit A cutaway -> living + kitchen + master -> materials in 3 categories -> finish packages and A/B compare -> rooftop wellness pool and gym -> cafe/restaurant -> Material Board PDF summary -> demo inquiry (clearly local only). Show analytics/CRM as future concept, not working feature.

## Trust signals
At every stage explain concept drawings/renders vs real built photographs. Make labels subtle but visible. No false "only 2 units left", fake buyers/testimonials, fake rating, fake developer accreditation, fake energy certification or unverified origin claims. Avoid using real company branding as association or endorsement. Include original concept/legal footer.

## Price and product packaging for a future agency (not in current website checkout)
Offer the finished demo as an example of customizable enterprise-style real estate platform. Commercial proposal variants: Showroom Basic (high-end exterior and floor selection), Interactive Premium (full apartment/material configurator), Enterprise (real property data, CMS/CRM, multiple towers). Do not put actual prices without market research, project costing and contracted scope. Preserve implementation modules so reusable across customers, reducing future custom-project effort.

## Delivery pack for prospective client
Optional promotional <60-second screen capture of working interface> recorded with original permitted music; one-page explainer showing functionality, pilot requirements (DWG/Revit/IFC/3D scans, finish catalogs, brand/plan rights, pricing, content approval), sample supported devices and expected asset production schedule only if estimates researched. Show user actual preview in browser for strongest persuasion, not just pre-rendered video.
