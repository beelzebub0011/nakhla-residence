# FREE WALK & CAMERA GRAMMAR — FROM STREET TO EVERY ACCESSIBLE ROOM

## Required user promise
The user wants to physically enter the building, floors, rooms and amenities and **walk/look around freely** like a premium guided architectural experience. A row of disconnected still images, auto-spinning panorama or cut to a static image is not an acceptable substitute for this feature. Implement actual navigable 3D spaces for the hero unit and all showcased amenity areas; other units may share consistent template geometry at appropriate fidelity, explicitly documented as representative rather than 18 truly unique authored interiors. The user should be able to discover all 12 levels via DOM controls, including vertical movement to amenity floors, and free-walk the rooms provided in the implemented 3D plans. The existence of a room in the schedule MUST map to a safe view mode even if detailed furnishing is not finished; don't falsely pretend a still is freewalk.

## Modes (one camera state machine, not conflicting controls)
A. `Cinematic Tour`: optional auto-directed overview; skippable, interruptible, accessible, separate from free movement. A short tour stops smoothly at exterior, floor, dollhouse, living, material inspector, wellness, pool and rooftop café; if assets missing branch to documented fallback, not broken camera animation.
B. `Orbit Exterior`: constrained orbit around tower, zoom bounds, smooth inertia, pan limit, no below-ground camera, prevents clipping through entire tower accidentally; floor raycast and semantic list synchronized.
C. `Floor Cutaway`: exterior zoom aligns to selected level, floor plate rises/isolates, wall/shell fades or hides without changing floor IDs; user orbits floor plan, chooses unit A/B, then a specific room.
D. `Dollhouse`: 3D overhead tilted furnished unit with walls cut/roof removed selectively. Room hotspots and labels are accessible DOM controls and clickable 3D targets; coherent floorplan and door placement.
E. `Guided Interior`: default easy mode; tap room labels/map -> smoothly teleport to sensible camera bookmarks with optional short walk transition. Works great on mobile without joystick frustration.
F. `Free Walk`: opt-in first-person continuous movement inside real modeled accessible geometry; look around, step through openable doors, visit adjacent rooms, discover window/balcony; keep camera from clipping/moving through solid walls. User may exit walk mode instantly, jump to selected room, or press Home.
G. `Accessibility/Lite`: semantic plans, material panels, labelled photos with full equivalent information; no promises of actual 3D movement if hardware lacks WebGL.

## Walk and physics requirements
Use documented camera eye height starting ~1.60-1.70m with accessible preset variations, realistic walk speed (~1-1.6m/s baseline) with user control, limited acceleration and damping, zero head bob by default, no camera roll. Use navmesh + swept collision or robust simple capsule collider, static wall meshes and door passage tags. Do not implement expensive full physics for furniture when simple collision proxies suffice. Avoid walking through walls, bed, glass, handrails, pool water, floors, furniture or closed doors; ensure floating/falling out of world cannot occur. Staircase/corridor collision treated consistently; for 12-level traversal, **accessible elevator menu/teleport** is preferred over demanding users physically climb stairs. Actual elevator animation may be a shallow optional transition but its choices must be navigable, matching floor list. Do not let a 3D character fall from balcony/roof; barriers and safe bounds needed.
- Room positions derived from canonical room polygons/nav graph; collision bounds and eye-level camera anchors match geometry; no entering mechanically inaccessible rooms or ceiling voids. For impossible narrow spaces, provide orbit/bookmark view rather than fake walk. Restricted plant/services zones marked not publicly explorable.
- Door interactions: on click/tap or approach prompt, rotate around a real pivot and maintain physical clearance; door never blocks navmesh without recalculation or state. Disable door opening effect if it causes clipping, default to open door. Transparent glass should be visually detectable; collision frame should match.
- Stairs/elevator: ensure accessible and coherent on concept plans. Elevator menu opens a list G,01..11 and highlights current level; entering selected floor never loads all floor interiors. If user clicks stair and no full 3D stair traversal, show honest transition to next level, not fake functional staircase.
- Walk input: WASD/arrows desktop, dragging mouse for look only after user engages walkthrough (pointer lock with clear exit if used), gamepad optional and not mandatory; touch virtual dual-stick is optional with polished design, tap-to-move/waypoint essential mobile fallback; thumb joystick must not obscure UI, adjustable sensitivity. Prevent keyboard shortcuts during forms. Treat desktop touchscreens as hybrid.
- Tap-to-move: Raycast navigable floor, select target waypoint, highlight path and ease camera to the point using validated navigation; no teleport behind closed door or into walls. Long press/gesture aim should not hijack browser navigation. For very weak devices prefer fixed camera anchor and smart room-hop instead of stutter.

## Navigation map and route grammar
A persistent miniature plan with current position, compass, entry direction, doors, and room labels; selectable destinations include Living, Kitchen, Master Bedroom, Second Bedroom, Bath, Balcony, Lobby, Gym, Indoor Pool, Sauna, Cafe, Restaurant and Terrace as appropriate. Map toggles accessible; on small phones a floating `Map` icon opens bottom sheet. Breadcrumb example: Nakhla Residence > Floor 04 > Unit 04A > Kitchen > Countertop. Buttons Back / Exit Walk / Floor List / Home remain visible and work after refresh/deep link. Ensure high-value demo never takes more than ~2 clear navigation choices from overview to living, and from current room to material inspector (measure during UX testing).

## Floor/unit room completeness and truthfulness
- 01-09 are real selectable floors, with coherent A/B locations. All 18 apartment selections show floor/unit data and at minimum an actual navigable minimal geometry using shared type templates, plus room navigation; if only hero home detailed, label others `Representative layout preview` and refrain from unique furnishing/photorealism claims. Better a common parametric geometry with varied orientation and views than 18 disconnected fake screenshots.
- F10 gym, pool and recreation must include actual interactive 3D walkable floor area; designate pool edge barriers and no swimming simulation; water material scalable/reflection alternate. F11 restaurant/cafe/terrace must be physically navigable, including separate (conditional) shisha area, usable furniture paths and safe railings. If constraints demand limited interiors, scope release honestly and never show inactive controls pretending a scene exists.
- Exterior path from street: camera can focus/drop-off, approach lobby, navigate lobby area with actual interior surfaces, optionally enter elevator to Floor 04; users can also jump directly from floor picker. The handoff should feel continuous without forcing one huge 12-story model resident in GPU memory.

## Camera transition director
Store cameraAnchor IDs per floor/room and semantic entrance/exit target; each transition has `from`, `to`, duration range, easing, collision-safe bezier/spline or direct fade, and cancellation/resume. Never animate camera through walls/furniture just because a mathematical curve looks stylish. For distant levels use short transition with tasteful cut/fade, preserving spatial orientation via mini map and floor labels. Sync UI navigation state only after scene target is ready, with reliable rollback on load errors. Use per-gesture cancellation tokens. For reduced motion, instantly switch to target frame and remove background animations. Frame budget manager may downgrade post effects during camera movement to maintain FPS, then improve on idle.

## Scene object interaction
Raycast priority: material-surface hotspots if inspector enabled > room target hotspot > floor selection > decoration; do not let weather particles, glass panes or invisible shadow meshes intercept clicks. Hit targets with enlarged bounds on touch; provide consistent hover/selected states and 2D button equivalence. Selecting carpet/wood/wall shows exact scene surface ID, catalog metadata and actual selected material. A wrong room surface is a P0 bug. On transitions, close panels that are scene-specific while preserving changed finishes and providing undo. Camera must not jump on scrolling info drawer. User can return from Material Inspector to exactly previous camera pose.

## Camera artifact deliverables
- `NAV_GRAPH.json` with rooms/floors/door adjacency, collision & camera anchor IDs.
- `CAMERA_BOOKMARKS.json`, `SHOT_LIST.md`, optional guided timeline JSON, movement control manual for touch/mouse/keyboard.
- Screen capture/video of real 3D freewalk through Living -> Kitchen -> Hall -> Master (as applicable) AND F10 Gym->Pool AND F11 Café->Terrace; these must be actual browser captures, not Blender flythrough represented as interactive.
- QA report: collision tests, 15 rapid route selections, 10 enter/exit walk cycles, camera loss after tab resume, device rotate, reduced motion, missing model and fallback.

## Release gates
No deployment as full freewalk demo if selected walk mode is a fake still, user cannot leave walk mode, floor selection becomes stale, camera passes through solid wall/ground, mobile person can't move to an adjacent room, navigation state resets on weather/quality changes, or amenity zones crash. A labelled guided-room alternative is acceptable on low-end devices and must preserve content parity.
