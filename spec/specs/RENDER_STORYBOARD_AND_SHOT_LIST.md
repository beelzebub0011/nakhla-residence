## Camera & metadata for every shot
scene_id, shot_id, local camera position xyz, target xyz, nominal lens/FOV, aspect, resolution, source .blend, rendered package, daylight state, texture pack version, asset license owner, image filename, alt text AR/EN, what it proves, reviewer score. Material comparison uses SAME camera and lighting. Render final via reliable antialias/denoise and inspect 100% zoom; no floating objects, repeated UVs, clipped railings or overbright glass. Composition must crop gracefully on 16:9 and 9:16 or create two purposeful crop variants.

## Camera movement storyboard
T01 Hero: very slow dolly/orbit until subject understood; CTA never hidden.
T02 Floor hover: slight silhouette highlight no cinematic cut.
T03 Floor select: 0.8–1.8s easing zoom/extract with stable orientation.
T04 Unit select: floor plate develops into cutaway and selected boundary glows delicately.
T05 Room enter: position and target animate via wall-safe path or match-cut, ending in visually beautiful frame.
T06 Material selection: room stays anchored to show clear before/after; no distractive camera.
T07 Back out: inverse of T05 and T04 where plausible, no memory loss.
T08 Amenities: elevator-inspired spatial focus to upper level, scene swap clearly contextual.
T09 Guided tour end: return to tower hero or finish board, no endless autoplay.
